Good to know

Questions, answered

Where do you deliver?

We deliver anywhere within 9 road-km of our Hosur hub. At checkout, drop a GPS pin or map pin — we calculate your exact road distance and confirm the route before your slot.

What are the delivery slots and fees?

Two morning slots daily: 7:00–9:00 AM and 9:00–11:00 AM. Fees start at ₹20 for 0–1 km and scale by road distance up to ₹100 for 7–9 km. Delivery is free on baskets over ₹500.

Is Cash on Delivery available?

Yes — COD is fully live on every order. Online UPI/card payments unlock soon; we will announce them on WhatsApp.

Is there a minimum order?

No minimum. Order a single bunch of coriander or a full family combo — the rider brings whatever you need.

How fresh is the produce, really?

Vegetables are harvested from farms around Hosur the same morning and packed by hand before sunrise. Nothing is cold-stored or re-routed through warehouses.

Can I track or change my order?

Yes. Use the Track order page with your order ID and mobile number. To change anything, message us on WhatsApp — we confirm changes before packing starts.
